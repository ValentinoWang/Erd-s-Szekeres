# Project overview

The project studies the open `ES(7)=33` case through rank-3 signotopes, exact local relations, proof-producing SAT, and single-element extension obstructions.

The investigation began by freezing a canonical orientation-variable encoding and an adversarial proof harness. It then developed along three coordinated routes:

- **Route A — local mathematical structure:** tight paths, prime implicates, propagation-complete OBDDs, and exact eight/nine-element window dynamics.
- **Route B — extension obstructions:** guarded-Horn reachability, exact low-width projections, shortest-path blockers, and factorized Logic-Benders circuits.
- **Route C — global proof production:** compact exact masters, strengthening clauses with replayable proofs, solver portfolios, cube covers, and DRAT/LRAT interfaces.

The research deliberately separates theorem discovery from theorem acceptance. Candidate statements are promoted only after the scope, hypotheses, and evidence are frozen. Failed routes are retained because they identify structural barriers: for example, the exact nine-point overlap graph is strongly connected, so fixed consecutive-window extinction through that state space cannot finish the proof.
