# Route A — local structure and transition dynamics

Route A replaces raw SAT search with finite mathematical objects that can be completely classified.

## Components

1. Tight-path propagation and convexity characterizations.
2. Complete prime-implicate layers on five, six, and seven labels.
3. A propagation-complete six-clause ITE encoding of reduced OBDDs.
4. Exact convex-seven-free relations on eight and nine labels.

## Main barrier identified

The exact eight-state / nine-transition digraph is strongly connected. Therefore a proof strategy that retains only a consecutive eight-element state and waits for transition extinction cannot establish `ES(7)=33`. Additional long-range orientation information is necessary.
