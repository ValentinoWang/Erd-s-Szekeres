Motivated by the open case ES(7)=33 of the planar Erdos-Szekeres problem, we prove four structural theorem families for convex-seven-free rank-3 signotopes. First, a tight-path propagation theorem shows that a monochromatic sequence of consecutive triple orientations forces every triple on the sequence to have the same sign. On seven elements this yields an exact complementary-path characterization of convexity and a family of fork, ladder, bridge, and Horn-dynamic-program obstructions.

Second, we determine the complete seven-element implication theory through width six. There are 393,988 width-six prime implicates, all contained in the layered unit-propagation closure of the four-point axioms and the complete five- and six-point prime layers. A propagation-complete reduced OBDD then represents every local implicate of arbitrary width.

Third, we determine the exact overlap dynamics of convex-seven-free windows. The eight-element relation has 1,221,504 states and the nine-element relation has 108,068,130 models. The induced transition digraph has 59,441,346 edges, is strongly connected, and contains a directed two-cycle. This identifies the information lost by fixed consecutive-window arguments.

Finally, for every fixed 32-element prefix, adjoining a rightmost element is exactly a guarded-Horn reachability problem, equivalently a fixed-prefix 2-SAT instance. Minimal prefix obstructions are the inclusion-minimal label unions of source-to-sink paths. We determine the obstruction spectrum through width four, prove that width four is insufficient, classify the width-five mechanisms, and give exact factorization and finite-convergence theorems for shared reachability circuits. These results reduce the signotope relaxation of the 33-point case to a finite global extension decision.
